Privacy Policy

Box-It · Last updated 21 August 2026

Box-It stores your progress on your own device. The app has no servers, makes no network requests, and has no way to identify you.

Box-It is a single-player maths puzzle game published by swag guy inc (“we”, “us”).

What we store on your device

The app writes one save file to your device’s private app storage:

StoredExample
Level progresshighest level reached, stars and best score per level
Liveshow many remain, and when the next one regenerates
Settingssound on/off, vibration on/off, volume level
Cosmeticswhich board themes you own and which is active
Statisticslevels completed, best chain length, best endless score

This file never leaves your device. We cannot read it. Deleting the app deletes it. There is no account, no login and no cloud backup — which also means progress does not transfer to a new device.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise or coarse location, or any advertising identifier. The app requests no runtime permissions, and uses no cookies or analytics.

Children

Box-It is suitable for all ages and is not directed at any specific age group. Because the app collects no personal information at all, no information is knowingly collected from children.

Your rights

Because nothing is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, export or delete. Removing the app removes everything the game has stored. Questions: realoblivion2017@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds advertising, purchases or analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is released and the date above will change.
