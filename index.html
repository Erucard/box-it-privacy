Privacy Policy - Box-It

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Box-It is a maths puzzle game published by RESO STUDIOS LIMITED. Contact us at support@resostudios.co.uk.

Summary

Your progress and settings are stored locally. We do not run a player-account service or a game-data server. Ad-supported builds use Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads. Board themes are currently free; the game does not offer in-app purchases.

Information stored on your device

The local save contains level progress, stars, scores, lives, settings, the selected theme, and gameplay statistics. We do not automatically receive this save. Box-It does not provide its own cloud-save or cross-device transfer service. Device backup and restore behaviour is controlled by your operating system.

Optional rewarded advertising

Ad-supported builds offer ads in exchange for a life or a continue. Videos are shown only when you choose to watch one. Once Google's consent framework allows ad requests, the advertising SDK may initialise and preload an ad before you tap. Builds labelled FINAL BETA 0.1 with the free-beta configuration do not initialise AdMob or request ads; their continue/retry rewards are free.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may process IP addresses and approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic information such as crash logs and performance data. See the Google Privacy Policy, Google's partner-services information, and Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.

Google's consent framework determines whether a consent message is needed. Where available, use Settings → Privacy Options to review your choices. On iOS, tracking permission is separate and can be managed in the device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Refusing tracking does not prevent you from playing. An optional ad reward may be unavailable if consent, network access, or an ad is unavailable.

Other information and your choices

We do not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, or precise location, and we do not include a separate analytics SDK. Advertising SDK diagnostics are described above. If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send and use them to handle your request. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

Google describes its retention, international processing, and privacy controls in the policies linked above. Contact us about information you send directly to RESO STUDIOS LIMITED, including access or deletion requests. Applicable privacy rights depend on where you live. We do not sell information you send to support.

Children

Box-It is intended for teens and adults and is not directed at children. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent personal information to us, please contact us so we can address it. Device parental controls can restrict app access and other services.

Changes

We will update this policy when the game's data practices change and revise the date above. Questions: support@resostudios.co.uk.
