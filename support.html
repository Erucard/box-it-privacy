Box-It Support

Published by RESO STUDIOS LIMITED.

Need help? Email support@resostudios.co.uk.

Please include your phone model, iOS or Android version, the game version shown in Settings, and what happened. A screenshot can help. Do not send passwords or payment details.

How do I play?

Open Settings and choose How to Play. Connect neighbouring numbers, choose operators, then place the equals sign on the answer. Multiplication and division are evaluated before addition and subtraction.

Are themes free?

Yes. Mint, Forest dark, Paper and Midnight are all free. Choose one from Themes.

Why is an ad unavailable?

Rewarded ads need an internet connection and an available ad. Try again later. You can retry a level or start a new Endless run without watching an ad. A failed daily attempt remains locked until the next day unless you complete a rewarded ad.

Where is my progress?

Progress is stored locally. Box-It has no game account or cloud save service, so support cannot restore deleted progress or transfer it to another device.
